GX4000 Tang Nano 20K project

MAIN PHASE 4 BUILD:
  GX4000_Phase4_HDMI.gprj

Signal path:
  Real cartridge
    -> Phase 2 Z80/RAM/CRTC
    -> Phase 3 GX4000 Plus ASIC
    -> Phase 4 AY/PPI/controller subsystem
    -> 720x576p50 HDMI/TMDS + 48 kHz stereo LPCM

Controller note:
  Phase-4 logical joystick/ADC inputs are implemented.
  The standard HDMI wrapper leaves them inactive until a reviewed
  physical controller-harness GPIO pinout is chosen.
